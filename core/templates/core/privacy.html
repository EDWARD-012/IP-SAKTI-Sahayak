Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  |  Version 4.3

1. What We Collect

IP-SAKTI Sahayak is designed to minimise personal data collection in alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

  • Query fingerprint: We store a keyed HMAC-SHA256 hash of your question — not the question text itself. This fingerprint cannot be reversed to recover your query.
  • Session data: Your last 4 conversation turns are stored temporarily in your browser session for context continuity. This data expires with your session and is never written to our permanent database.
  • Audit metadata: Request ID, corpus version used, jurisdiction selected, response outcome code, citation IDs, and response latency are stored for quality monitoring.
  • No personal identifiers: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, Aadhaar, or any other personal identifier unless you volunteer them in the contact form.

2. DPDP Act 2023 Alignment

This application implements the following DPDP-aligned practices:

  • Data minimisation: Only data necessary for service quality monitoring is stored.
  • Purpose limitation: Audit data is used solely for improving answer quality and system monitoring.
  • No query text storage: The HMAC key is rotated per deployment. Even with access to the database, your questions cannot be reconstructed.
  • Session controls: You can delete your session data at any time using the Delete Session control in the chat interface.

3. Cookies and Sessions

This application uses a single session cookie to maintain your language preference, conversation context, and wizard progress. No tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party advertising cookies are used.

4. Data Retention

  • Session data: Deleted when your session expires (7 days) or when you click "Delete session".
  • Audit metadata: Retained for system monitoring as per deployment policy.

5. Legal Disclaimer

IP-SAKTI Sahayak provides general information only. It does not constitute legal advice. Always verify information with official sources and consult a qualified IP professional before acting.
