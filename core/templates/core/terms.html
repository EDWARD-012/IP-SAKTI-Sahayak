Important Notice

IP-SAKTI Sahayak provides general information only. It is not a substitute for legal advice from a qualified Intellectual Property practitioner or legal professional. Do not act solely on the basis of information obtained from this system.

1. General Information Only

All answers generated by IP-SAKTI Sahayak are based on a static, versioned corpus of Indian statutes, rules, and international treaties. The system does not provide:

  • Legal advice or legal opinions
  • Advice on pending legislation or recent amendments not in the active corpus
  • Jurisdiction-specific advice for countries other than India
  • Tax, commercial, or financial advice

2. Corpus Accuracy Limitations

The legal corpus is updated periodically. Laws change frequently. Always:

  • Check the corpus version and last-updated date shown on the About Corpus page.
  • Verify citations against official published sources (India Code, e-Gazette, official ministry publications).
  • Check the effective date of each cited provision — laws may have been amended after the corpus was last indexed.

3. Acceptable Use

  • Use the service for research, education, and general IP information purposes.
  • Do not attempt to extract or reproduce the corpus content for commercial redistribution.
  • Do not use the service to attempt prompt injection, jailbreaking, or bypassing safety checks.
  • Do not submit queries containing personal identification information of others.
