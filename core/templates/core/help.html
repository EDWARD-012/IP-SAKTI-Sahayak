{# ── How to use the Chat ── #}

How to Use the Ask IP-SAKTI Chat

  1. Go to Ask IP-SAKTI.
  2. Select the Jurisdiction: India for Indian law, International for treaty-level questions, or Both.
  3. Type your question in the text box. Be specific — e.g. "Can I patent an Ayurvedic formulation based on Charak Samhita?"
  4. Click Ask or press Ctrl+Enter.
  5. The system retrieves relevant legal text and generates a cited answer.
  6. Review the Evidence Label and open each citation to verify the source.
{# ── Evidence Labels ── #}

Understanding Evidence Labels

Strong evidence

The important claims have direct, current support in verified corpus excerpts. All citations are validated — the section reference appears verbatim in the retrieved chunk.

Limited evidence

Support is partial, qualified, or some citations could not be fully validated. Inspect the cited text carefully before relying on this answer.

Unable to answer

The active corpus cannot safely support an answer. This is a refusal, not a negative legal conclusion. The law may exist but is not yet in this corpus version, or the question is outside scope.

{# ── Citations ── #}

How to Read Citations

Each citation shows:

  • Source title — e.g. "The Patents Act, 1970"
  • Section reference — e.g. "Section 3(p)"
  • Effective from / amended — the date the provision became effective
  • Quoted text — the verbatim excerpt from the corpus; this is NOT generated by the AI

Step for verification: Check the section number and effective date, then open the official source link (India Code, e-Gazette, WIPO LEXIS) to confirm the provision is still in force as cited.

{# ── Formulation Wizard ── #}

Using the Formulation Wizard

The Formulation Wizard helps you identify the most relevant IP protection paths for your product through 4 guided questions:

  1. Product type — Classical formulation, novel recipe, medicinal plant, extract, cosmetic, or food.
  2. Your intent — Protect, commercialise, prevent bio-piracy, understand rights, or export.
  3. Jurisdiction — India, international, or both.
  4. Existing IP — Whether you already have any registered IP protection.

The wizard results show recommended IP paths with links to ask specific questions about each.

{# ── FAQ ── #}

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does it sometimes say "Unable to answer from verified sources"?

This happens when the active corpus does not have enough relevant text to safely support the answer. It is not a statement that no law exists — it means the current corpus version cannot answer this question reliably. Try rephrasing, changing jurisdiction, or checking the corpus coverage on the About page.

Are the answers legally binding?

No. All answers are general information only, not legal advice. Consult a qualified IP attorney or the relevant authority (IPO India, NBA, FSSAI) before making legal or commercial decisions.

Is my question stored or logged?

No. Your question text is never stored in the database. We store only a keyed cryptographic fingerprint (HMAC-SHA256) that cannot be reversed. Your conversation history exists only in your session and is deleted when you clear it or when the session expires. See our Privacy Policy.

What languages are supported?

The interface supports all 22 Eighth Schedule languages plus English. For non-English questions, the system translates to English for retrieval and the answer is provided in English with an option to view in the selected language. Note that for low-resource languages (Santali, Dogri, Bodo, Maithili), translation quality is marked as Pilot/Beta.
